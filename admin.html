The official Job Order Board layout is detected automatically (notes at the top, company name rows, and job rows underneath). The simple one-row-per-job format below also works.

Your spreadsheet should have a header row with these column names (exact match, case-insensitive). All columns are optional except Company and Position.

PositionJob title (required)

Work ModeIn Person | Remote | (leave blank)
Openinge.g. May 1 | 2026-05-01 | (blank = not listed)
Closinge.g. Jun 30 | OUF | NCD | (blank = not listed)
ContactContact person name
EmailEmail address or phone number
LinkFull URL to job posting
ExpiredYES / TRUE / 1 = expired; NO / FALSE / 0 or blank = open
